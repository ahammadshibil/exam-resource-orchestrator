# Third-party reference metadata

## HackerTube catalog

This package includes a reduced reference-metadata index from **HackerTube**, https://hackertube.org, created by Andrej Karpathy. Source export: https://hackertube.org/data, published 31 October 2026 and retrieved 7 November 2026. Official schema and notices: https://hackertube.org/download/DATA.md.

The catalog is derived from **open-index/hacker-news**, https://huggingface.co/datasets/open-index/hacker-news. Hacker News data is provided by Y Combinator; comments remain the work of their authors. No comment texts or quotes are included in this reduced index.

This module is an independent integration, not an endorsement by or affiliation with HackerTube, Andrej Karpathy, Hacker News, Y Combinator, YouTube or Google.

Transformation: retain only exported video IDs, original YouTube links, catalog titles, channel names and topic labels. Omit summaries, comments, quotations, HN recommendation counts, community scores, embeddings and co-recommendation graphs. Ranking does not use community popularity. Video titles and channel names belong to their creators; the database license is not a license to the videos themselves. No video, audio, transcript or thumbnail content is distributed here.

Catalog labels and titles are source metadata, not independently verified teaching content. Playback availability, language, cost, age/region restrictions, educational accuracy and examination alignment remain unverified until separately checked. Each returned HackerTube reference carries attribution and an original-video link.
