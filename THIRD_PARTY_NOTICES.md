# Third-party reference metadata

## Publisher locator metadata

Version 0.3.0 includes bibliographic and locator metadata from OpenStax (https://openstax.org), NPTEL (https://nptel.ac.in) and MIT OpenCourseWare (https://ocw.mit.edu), collected 7 November 2026 from publisher-advertised catalogs and sitemaps. Exact source listing/sitemap URLs accompany each record. NPTEL titles are publisher catalog metadata; OpenStax/MIT locator labels are generated from publisher URLs and are not claimed as copied page headings. Publisher names, course titles and other marks remain their owners'.

Only titles/locators, subject labels and source references are included. No book chapter, lecture, media, transcript, illustration or exercise body is copied. Robots access and public linking are not represented as a teaching-content reuse license. Resource license fields remain unknown/link-only unless independently verified. Provider directory entries likewise do not grant access, copying rights or endorsement. Collection details and restrictions are documented in [docs/INDEX_SOURCES.md](docs/INDEX_SOURCES.md).

## HackerTube catalog

This package includes a reduced reference-metadata index from **HackerTube**, https://hackertube.org, created by Andrej Karpathy. Source export: https://hackertube.org/data, published 31 October 2026 and retrieved 7 November 2026. Official schema and notices: https://hackertube.org/download/DATA.md.

The catalog is derived from **open-index/hacker-news**, https://huggingface.co/datasets/open-index/hacker-news. Hacker News data is provided by Y Combinator; comments remain the work of their authors. No comment texts or quotes are included in this reduced index.

The reference database is licensed under the **Open Data Commons Attribution License (ODC-By) v1.0**: https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/by/1-0/. This notice and source attribution must accompany redistribution of the included metadata. This module is an independent integration, not an endorsement by or affiliation with HackerTube, Andrej Karpathy, Hacker News, Y Combinator, YouTube or Google.

Transformation: retain only exported video IDs, original YouTube links, catalog titles, channel names and topic labels. Omit summaries, comments, quotations, HN recommendation counts, community scores, embeddings and co-recommendation graphs. Ranking does not use community popularity. Video titles and channel names belong to their creators; the database license is not a license to the videos themselves. No video, audio, transcript or thumbnail content is distributed here.

Catalog labels and titles are source metadata, not independently verified teaching content. Playback availability, language, cost, age/region restrictions, educational accuracy and examination alignment remain unverified until separately checked. Each returned HackerTube reference carries attribution and an original-video link.
